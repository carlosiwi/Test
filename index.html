About

I'm a Product Designer from Córdoba, Spain. My background spans graphic design, audiovisual production, 3D and web design — which means I'm as comfortable sweating the visual details as I am mapping a service blueprint.

What drives me is impact: I'd rather ship a focused improvement that moves a metric than a beautiful concept that never leaves Figma. I work best embedded in product teams, pairing daily with PMs and engineers.

Away from the screen you'll find me at the cinema, drawing, painting, running or playing basketball — and endlessly photographing architecture.
